## No GPU barbecue warranty

Experimental software, without warranty or liability to the extent allowed by
law. Keep power and thermal protections enabled. Stop if your system becomes
unstable. The GPU-fire joke is not a test plan.

## Privacy and submissions

- Report checks and image previews stay in your browser. Uploading to GitHub is manual.
- Opening a local report keeps its sanitized summary in this tab's session storage until the tab closes.
- The summary excludes local paths and startup arguments. **Your ZIP is not sanitized:** review it yourself.
- GitHub submissions are public, including hardware, settings, timings, optional GPU sensor charts and your GitHub account.
- No site analytics or tracking cookies. GitHub hosting and attachments follow GitHub's privacy policy.
- Donations take place on PayPal. This site does not collect payment details or embed PayPal scripts.
- The optional intro loads YouTube's privacy-enhanced player only after you click. Once loaded, YouTube's privacy policy applies.
